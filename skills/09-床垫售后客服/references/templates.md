# 英文站内信模板

## 使用说明

这些模板只是基础骨架，不是固定回复。生成邮件前先读取 `calibration-memory.md`，如果用户校准规则适用于当前场景，应优先按校准规则调整：

- 语气：更自然、更简洁、更像美国客服。
- 信息顺序：先要证据、先安抚、先查物流、先说明政策边界等。
- 避免表达：用户明确纠正过的词句以后不要再写。

不要因为模板中出现某一句话就机械复用。每次都要结合客户原话、订单状态、风险点和用户校准规则重新组织回复。

## 通用结构

```text
Subject: Re: [Issue] - Order #[Order ID]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us. We are sorry to hear about the issue with your mattress.

[Empathy + brief explanation]

[Action / resolution path]

[Next step requested from customer or next action by seller]

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```

## 物流延误

```text
Subject: Re: Delivery Status - Order #[Order ID]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for reaching out. We are sorry that your mattress has not arrived as expected.

We will check the latest tracking status with the carrier. If you have already received any update from the carrier, please reply with a screenshot so we can review it together with the order details.

Once we confirm the current status, we will help you with the best available solution according to our store policy.

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```

## 未完全膨胀

```text
Subject: Re: Mattress Expansion - Order #[Order ID]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for contacting us. We are sorry that the mattress has not expanded as expected.

Because compressed mattresses may need time to fully recover after unpacking, could you please confirm when the mattress was opened and how many hours it has been lying flat in a ventilated room?

Please also send us a photo showing the current height measurement and the product label if available. We will review the details and help you with the next step according to our store policy.

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```

## 异味

```text
Subject: Re: Mattress Odor - Order #[Order ID]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for letting us know. We are sorry for the inconvenience caused by the odor after unpacking.

Compressed mattresses may have a temporary smell after being sealed for shipping. We recommend placing the mattress in a well-ventilated room and allowing it to air out.

If the odor remains strong after ventilation, please let us know how long the mattress has been unpacked and send us any photos of the product label. We will review the case and assist you with the best available solution according to our store policy.

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```

## 太软或太硬

```text
Subject: Re: Mattress Comfort - Order #[Order ID]

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for sharing your experience with us. We are sorry that the mattress does not feel comfortable for you.

Firmness can feel different depending on sleep position, body type, bed frame, and personal preference. To better understand the issue, could you please let us know how long you have used the mattress and what type of bed frame or base it is placed on?

After we review the details, we will help you with the best available solution according to our store policy.

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```

```text
Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We sincerely apologize for the issue with your mattress.

To help us review this properly, could you please send us clear photos or a short video showing the issue, along with a photo of the product label? If this is a size issue, please include a photo showing the measurement.

Once we receive the information, we will review it promptly and help you with the best available solution according to our store policy.

Best regards,
[Store Name]
```
